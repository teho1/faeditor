# Privacy Policy for FA Editor

**Last updated:** 20 September 2026

FA Editor (“the app”) is a Studio Set editor for Roland FA-06 / FA-07 / FA-08 keyboards on macOS, iPad, and iPhone. This policy describes what information the app handles.

## Summary

FA Editor does **not** collect, sell, or share personal data. It does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking. Work stays on your device and on your connected instrument over USB or Bluetooth MIDI.

## Data the app stores on your device

The app may save files only on your computer or in the iOS app sandbox, for example:

- Local Studio Set library projects (JSON)
- Tone favourites and related preferences
- Optional crash-recovery drafts for unsaved work

You control these files. Uninstalling the app or deleting that folder removes them. They are not uploaded by the app.

## MIDI and USB

When you connect a Roland FA, the app exchanges MIDI SysEx with the instrument to read and write Studio Set and related parameters. That traffic stays between your device and the instrument. The app does not send MIDI data to external servers. On iPad and iPhone the editor pauses MIDI while it is in the background and reconnects when you return to the app.

## Network use

The app does not require an internet connection for normal editing. On iOS, Bluetooth permission is requested only if you connect a Bluetooth MIDI interface.

If you open Help/About links (for example GitHub or Qt licensing pages), macOS may open those URLs in your browser. That browsing is outside the app and subject to those sites’ own policies.

Building from source may download dependencies (such as RtMidi) via your development tools; that is not part of the App Store app’s runtime behaviour.

## Third parties

- **Apple** processes App Store purchase and account data under Apple’s privacy policy when you buy or download the app.
- **Roland** trademarks and instruments are independent of this app. The app is unofficial and not affiliated with Roland Corporation.

## Children

The app is a music utility and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

We may update this policy if the app’s behaviour changes. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.
